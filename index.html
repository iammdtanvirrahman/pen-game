How to play

  1. Every player gets one pen.
  2. Players take turns. Only the active pen can be flicked.
  3. Press your pen and drag backwards, like a slingshot.
  4. Release to flick. A longer drag is a harder flick.
  5. Knock opponents' pens off the bench.
  6. Keep your own pen on the bench.
  7. The last pen on the bench wins.

Optional house rules (Custom match): AI level, flick strength, aim guide. A pen is out when its centre crosses the bench edge; touching the edge is safe.
